SHARED CHARACTERS, ONE DRAWING EACH. The library cat is Mrs Crumpet, drawn as the `kitten` on her pile of books in both Shush and Storm. ### Duck, Sir Hopsalot!  (writer 1, FUNNY, book 0; id `sirHopsalot`)

*Sir Hopsalot, the grandest bunny in the park, pushes in at the Rail Race and challenges RiPika, then bonks every striped bar because he thinks “duck” means a duck, until RiPika gives up her lead to show him.*

**Adversary:** Sir Hopsalot, a pompous white bunny (the 'bunny' model) with the tallest ears in the park: a rival. He pushes in at the Rail Race queue, calls RiPika “small yellow person” and challenges her to a race, then bonks bar after bar because nobody ever told him that “duck” means get down low. Resolved kindly: RiPika could win easily but slows down to teach him; he ducks the last bar beside her; she wins by the tip of her nose; he keeps his promise to call her magnificent (and they argue happily about who is more magnificent); and the next day he gives her a rubber duck and queues at the back like everybody else.

**The judge's edits:** Text ships as written (28–34 words a page). Every model is already in PICTURE_MODELS; the fixes are all about position and scale. (1) A bare `cart` is built at RIDE_SCALE (2.5×, with the seat 2.4 m up), but `duckBar` is hung for `raceCart`'s park scale (bar centre about 2.7 m). Give both carts size 0.4 and seat RiPika and Sir Hopsalot at y ≈ 0.958 (SEAT_HEIGHT), or the bar cuts straight through them. (2) pose 'duck' only folds a raceCart's own rider, and pets are 1.46 m tall including ears. Sitting up, both of them already pass under the bar. So 'ducking' on p4, p5, p7 and p8 means sinking them into the tub with `at`, and on p4/p5 the gate must come down (negative y) until it really meets his ear tips. (3) Drop size 1.3 on the p7/p8 bar. Scaling the gate lifts the bar 30%, so it would hang above everyone and nobody would need to duck. Make it 'the biggest' by setting it nearer the camera instead. (4) His ears can't fold (the picture schema has no ear control), so p8 shows him sunk low with his ear tips just under the bar. Optional: p1 is all set-up; adding 'He even told the trees.' gives it a laugh at 35 words.
